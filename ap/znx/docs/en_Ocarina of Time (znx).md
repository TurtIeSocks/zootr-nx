# Ocarina of Time (znx)

## What does randomization do to this game?

The Switch 2 Ocarina of Time, randomized by zootr-nx. Every check in the shuffle (chests, gifts, scrubs and the Treasure Chest Game's prize) can hold any item from any game in the multiworld.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Ganon.

## Which items and locations are shuffled?

The default zootr-nx shuffle: the overworld's and dungeons' chests, NPC gifts and business scrubs. Dungeon keys, maps and compasses stay where they are for now.

## What does another world's item look like in this game?

Playing a seed in the game isn't supported yet: the zootr-nx app can't open a `.apznx` so far. When it can, a check holding another player's item will name the player it goes to.
